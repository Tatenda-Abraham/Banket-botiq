Fashion Hub catalogue

Update your products

There is no account or database to set up. Edit the products list in js/store.js to change what appears on the website.

Adding a photo

  1. Copy your photo into the website's images folder.
  2. Use its path in the product's image value, for example images/blue-dress.jpg.
  3. Save js/store.js and refresh the website.

Adding or changing a product

In js/store.js, edit a product's name, category, price, availability, and image. Copy a product object to add another item, or remove an object to take one off the catalogue.

{
  name: "Blue Summer Dress",
  category: "Dresses",
  price: 15,
  availability: "Available",
  image: "images/blue-dress.jpg"
}

Changing the currency or contact details

At the top of js/store.js, change currency to a currency code such as "BWP" or "ZAR". Fill in the phone, WhatsApp, email, and address values in contactDetails to display your contact information.
